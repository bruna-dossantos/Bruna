## The "Sen wants training + rehiring" Ask — How to Frame It

Sen's mandate is about **capability architecture**, not classroom training. The real questions:

1. Are current writers producing at the quality bar needed? If not — training problem or selection problem?
2. Can new hires ramp fast enough? No structured onboarding exists today — Stacey does it informally.
3. Is Stacey a bottleneck? Yes by design, but only works if she's leading not executing.

**The answer to "training" is three things:**
- **A competency model** — what does "good" look like at each writer level? Doesn't exist yet.
- **A feedback loop with teeth** — error taxonomy + calibration sessions + reviewer-as-gate = the mechanism.
- **A ramp protocol** — 5-10 ticket supervised review before solo, structured with Stacey.

**On rehiring clinical:** Frame to Sen as filling specific skill gaps, not headcount. Gaps are: respiratory + wound/ostomy/incon for DME, and two infusion writers. Those are the asks.
