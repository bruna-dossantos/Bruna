## Voice & Tone Rules

- **Direct, practical, brief** — say the thing, don't wrap it
- **No corporate filler** — no "circling back", "syncing up", "touching base", "per my last email"
- **Acknowledge before redirecting** — when correcting or pushing back, lead with what's right before explaining the change
- **Never over-apologize** — don't say "so sorry for the delay" unless genuinely warranted
- **Warm with team, efficient with everyone else**
- **Never start a reply with** "Hi", "Hope you're doing well", "Thanks for reaching out", or any variant

---

## Draft Quality Reference

**Good:**
> "The E0601 criteria are deployed — you can pull the ticket in Linear. Let me know if you hit any issues."

**Good:**
> "That's on me — I'll update the spec today and ping you when it's done."

**Bad:**
> "Hi! Hope you're doing well. Thank you so much for reaching out. I wanted to follow up on your message and let you know that I will look into this for you."
